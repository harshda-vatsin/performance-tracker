# MM Forgings Performance Portal: Questions We Need Answered

**For:** Corporate HR, Plant HR and Plant Heads, MM Forgings Ltd
**Purpose:** We cannot finalise the first release of the portal until these 12 questions are answered. Your answers decide how the portal calculates scores, who it shows to whom, and who signs off each month.

**How to answer:** A short note against each question is enough. Where it helps, please attach a sample Excel sheet or an example from a real month (names and numbers can be masked). If different plants do things differently, tell us which plants differ and how.

---

## A. Scoring

### 1. Is the scoring method the same in all 12 plants?
Today's sheets calculate a KRA score from the *last year's level*, the *target* and the month's *result*. Do all plants, Forge, Machine and the other three, use this same method? If any plant uses a different method, a different set of rules or a lookup table, please send us that sheet.
*Why we ask: if methods differ, we need to build each one, and we want the portal's numbers to match your sheets exactly.*

### 2. What score does a plant get for simply matching last year's level?
We understand this is **60** in Forge shops and **50** in Machine shops. Is that correct? What should it be for the three "Other" plants? Does any individual plant use a different figure?
*Why we ask: this one figure shifts every score in the system, so it must match your policy.*

### 3. What are the highest and lowest score a single KRA can get?
Some sheets currently show scores like 460 or −224 when a result is far from target. We suggest a lowest score of **0** and a highest of **120**. Do you agree, or would you choose other limits? Should the limits be the same for all KRAs?
*Why we ask: without limits, one unusual month can swamp the whole department's score and the bonus linked to it.*

### 4. What should happen in these special situations?
Please tell us your preferred treatment for each:
- **(a)** The target is the same as last year's level (for example, "keep delivery at 95%").
- **(b)** The target is zero (for example, "no lost-time accidents"), and the month has an accident.
- **(c)** A result is not available for a month (machine shut, data not received). Should the department wait for the data, count it as zero, or leave that KRA out and share its weight among the others?

*Why we ask: these cases come up every month, and the portal needs a clear rule so that no one is scored unfairly or has to guess.*

## B. Organisation structure

### 5. What are the departments and cells in each plant?
Please send the full list for all 12 plants: department names for Forge shops, and production cells for Machine shops (for example MAG, TML, ISUZU, M&M, Cross & Spider, Yoke & ZF). Also tell us which plants are the three "Other" types and roughly how they are organised.
*Why we ask: every score belongs to a department or cell, so the lists must be right before anything can be set up or loaded from your Excel sheets.*

### 6. Who holds each level in a department or cell, and how often does that change?
We understand the levels are HOD, Shift Incharge and Supervisor in Forge shops, and Cell Incharge, Setter & Supervisor and Staff in Machine shops. Is that right? Who decides which named person sits at which level each month? Can one person work in two departments or cells in the same month, and if so, how should their score be worked out?
*Why we ask: each person's bonus follows the score of their level, so we must know exactly who belongs where each month.*

### 7. Will every shop-floor worker be listed by name, and does everyone have an employee code?
Please confirm that permanent workers, contract workers and trainees all have a unique employee code, and that each person (not just the role) should appear in the portal. Do all of them have a mobile number we can use to send a one-time code for login?
*Why we ask: people log in with their employee code and mobile number, and bonuses are paid to named individuals.*

---

## C. Bonus and incentive

### 8. Who receives the monthly performance bonus and who receives the incentive?
Please tell us, for each group (staff, permanent workers, contract workers, trainees), whether they get the bonus, the incentive, both or neither. Also list the conditions that can stop a payment, for example minimum attendance days, a pending disciplinary matter, or notice period.
*Why we ask: the portal must apply the same eligibility conditions your HR team uses today, so nobody is paid or missed by mistake.*

### 9. How is the bonus or incentive amount worked out from the score?
For example: "score of 85 to 100 pays 100% of the base amount; 70 to 85 pays 70%". Please explain your current method, or share a worked example from a past month. Also tell us where the base amount for each person comes from (salary grade, a fixed table, or another source) and who keeps it up to date. How should part-month employees (joiners, leavers, long leave) be handled?
*Why we ask: this decides what the portal sends to payroll each month. We will build it so the rules can be changed later without rework, but we need your starting rules.*

---

## D. Approvals

### 10. What is the monthly timetable, and who checks what?
Please tell us the last date for each step (Plant HR sends data, HOD or Cell Incharge checks it, Plant Head approves, Corporate HR finalises, payroll receives the file). Should the HOD/Cell Incharge check each department or cell separately, and should the Plant Head approve each one or the whole plant in one go? Please also share the plants' holiday lists.
*Why we ask: reminders and escalations to the right person are built from this timetable, and late months need a clear owner.*

### 11. Who can change targets during the year, and who approves it?
If a plant wants to change a target or last year's level part-way through the year, who proposes it, who approves it, and from which month does it apply? Are targets set for each quarter, with every month in that quarter using the same target, or are there separate monthly targets?
*Why we ask: changed targets change scores. We want every change to have a named approver and a record, so results can always be explained later.*

### 12. If a month has been finalised and an error is found, who can reopen it?
Who may ask for a correction, and who must approve it? Is there a time limit (for example, only before payroll is released)? If the bonus has already gone to payroll, how should the correction be paid, as an adjustment in the next month? Should Corporate HR be allowed to enter data on a plant's behalf in an emergency?
*Why we ask: corrections after a month closes are sensitive because they affect pay, so the steps and the people responsible must be agreed in advance.*

---

*Thank you. Once we have your answers, we will confirm back in writing how each one has been built into the portal before work begins.*
